## The short version

We collect what GinMai needs to help you share a meal: your phone number, first name, the meals you post and join, and your location while you use the app. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics trackers. You can delete your account at any time in Settings.

## 2. What we collect

Account: your phone number (to sign in with a one-time code), your first name, your notification settings, and a push token for your device if you turn on notifications.

Meals ("Moments") you host: the time, the place (name, map position and area), number of seats, how long it lasts, and any note you add.

Taking part: which Moments you join, leave, arrive at, or say you're running late for.

Feedback: how a meal went and whether you'd eat with someone again. The other person doesn't see your answer; if you both say yes, you appear in each other's Connections.

Reliability: counts of meals hosted, meals joined and no-shows, shown on your profile.

Safety: people you block, and reports you make (reason, optional details, and the Moment).

Location: your device's location while you use the app, to centre the map, estimate walking distance, and place a Moment at "current location". We don't track you in the background. We store a location only as the place of a Moment you create.

We don't collect your contacts, photos, email, payment details, or advertising identifiers.

## 3. What other people see

Anyone signed in can see Moments on the map: the time, the place, the host's first name, seats, and note. People in the same Moment see each other's first names.

Your phone number, exact device location, feedback, blocks and reports are never shown to other users.

## 4. Why we use it

To run GinMai for you (performing our agreement with you): signing in, showing and joining meals, notifications you've asked for, and Connections.

To keep people safe (our legitimate interests): reliability signals, blocking, reviewing reports, and preventing people we've removed from coming back.

With your permission (consent): location and notifications. You can turn these off in your phone's settings at any time; some features then won't work.

## 7. How long we keep it

While your account exists, we keep your account data. Moments leave the map when they end.

When you delete your account, we delete right away: your profile, phone number, push token, the Moments you've joined, your feedback, blocks and Connections. Moments you hosted keep their time and place for the other guests, but your name and note are removed.
